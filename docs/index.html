MomentMap

A place is a fact. What happened there is a memory.

MomentMap is built for the memory, not the map pin. Browse by place or by day, keep every visit to somewhere as its own memory with its own photos and feeling, and see a whole day at once — where you went, in order, all on one page.

Privacy Policy Support

What it does

  • Map — browse your memories by where they happened.
  • Calendar — browse your memories by when they happened, as a day-by-day journal.
  • One place, many memories — every visit keeps its own photos, feeling, and note, so a place's whole history is there when you open it.
  • New Place — save a place by sharing a link from Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Yelp, typing a name, or letting your photos suggest it.
  • Photo Memory — turn a batch of photos into one or more Memories, reviewed before anything is saved.
  • A feeling, not a score — each memory gets its own feeling instead of a rating.
  • Share — send a Place or a Collection to another MomentMap user.
  • iCloud sync — your data syncs across your own devices automatically. No login, no sync button.
