Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. PlacePick has no login. Your data stays on your device and syncs privately across your own devices through your iCloud account.

Why does PlacePick ask for Photo Library access?

Only to read the date, time, and location of photos you choose to add to a Memory — never to scan your library automatically. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete a Place or Memory?

Open the Place or Memory and use the delete option in its detail screen. Deleted items are removed from your device and synced across your other devices.

How does sharing work?

You can share a saved Place or a whole Collection using the Share Sheet. The person receiving it chooses to add it as a new Collection or merge it into an existing one — your personal notes, emotions, and photos are never included in what's shared.
