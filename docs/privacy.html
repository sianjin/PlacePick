Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

PlacePick is built so that your places and memories stay yours. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

No account, no server

PlacePick does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address. There is no PlacePick server. Your data is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, synced privately across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit — the same private-database mechanism Apple provides to any app. Sianjin, the developer of PlacePick, cannot see, access, or receive your places, photos, notes, or any other content you create in the app.

What the app accesses, and why

PermissionWhy PlacePick asks
Location (While Using) To show your current position on the map relative to places you've saved, and to suggest nearby places when you're adding one. Your location is never sent anywhere outside the app.
Photo Library To read the capture date, time, and location metadata of photos you choose, so PlacePick can group them into Memories and suggest which place they belong to. PlacePick only reads photos you explicitly select — it never scans your library in the background, and it never uploads a photo anywhere. Photos remain in your Photos library; PlacePick keeps only a reference to the ones you've added to a Memory.

What PlacePick stores

PlacePick stores, on your device and in your own private iCloud database:

  • Places you save (name, coordinates, and identifier from Apple Maps)
  • Collections you create to organize places
  • Memories (visits) you record — including any Emotion or Note you add
  • References to photos you've attached to a Memory

None of this is linked to your identity in any way PlacePick controls, because PlacePick has no user accounts and no server to link it on. It is stored only in your own private iCloud container, governed by your own Apple ID and Apple's own iCloud privacy terms.

Sharing a Place or Collection

PlacePick lets you deliberately share a Place or a Collection with another person, using iOS's standard Share Sheet or the PlacePick Share Extension. This only happens when you explicitly choose to share — nothing is shared automatically, and only the place information you're sharing (name, coordinates, and the Collection's name/icon) is included. Your personal notes, emotions, and photos attached to that place are never included in a shared Collection.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

PlacePick does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites. It collects no usage analytics of any kind.

Third-party services

PlacePick uses Apple's MapKit for place search and Apple's CloudKit for sync — both governed by Apple's own privacy policy. If you choose to open a saved place in Google Maps, that action is handled entirely by the Google Maps app or website, governed by Google's privacy policy — PlacePick does not send Google anything before you make that choice.

Children's privacy

PlacePick does not knowingly collect information from children, and since it has no accounts, no server, and no data collection of any kind, no personal information is transmitted to or stored by the developer regardless of the user's age.
