Apple Maps already knows where a place is, what it's called, and how to get there.

PlacePick remembers why it mattered — how it felt, what happened there, and the photos that prove it.

PLACES, NOT JUST PINS
Save a place from Apple Maps and organize it into your own Collections — Coffee, Weekend Trips, Japan 2027, or however you actually think about your world. Not categories Apple Maps assigns you, but ones you choose.

MEMORIES, BUILT FROM YOUR PHOTOS
Pick a batch of photos and PlacePick groups them by time and location, suggests a place for each group, and lets you review everything — group boundaries, place, collection — in one screen before anything is saved. Nothing is created automatically. You always confirm.

BROWSE TWO WAYS
- Map: browse your saved places by where they are.
- Calendar: browse your memories by when they happened, as a day-by-day journal.

EMOTION OVER RATING
PlacePick never asks "how many stars?" It asks how a place made you feel — because memories aren't reviews.

SHARE WHAT MATTERS
Send a place or a whole collection to another PlacePick user. Receiving one never overwrites what you've already saved — your organization always wins.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your places and memories sync across your own devices with iCloud. No account, no login, no social feed. Nothing you save is visible to anyone unless you explicitly share it.

PlacePick is a personal memory layer built on top of Apple Maps — not another map app, not a review platform, not a social network. Just a quieter way to remember the places that mattered.
