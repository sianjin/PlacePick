A place is a fact. What happened there is a memory. MomentMap is built for the memory.

TWO WAYS TO HOLD A MEMORY
A memory has a place and a time, so MomentMap lets you find it either way.
- Space-first: the Map shows your memories by where they happened.
- Time-first: the Calendar shows your memories by when they happened, as a day-by-day journal.
Neither view is the "extra" one. Some memories you'll reach for by place, some by date — MomentMap keeps both equally close.

ONE PLACE, MANY MEMORIES
The same coffee shop, three visits over two years, are three different memories — not one entry that gets overwritten. Each visit keeps its own photos, its own feeling, its own note, so a place's whole history is there when you open it.

ONE DAY, MANY MEMORIES
Tap a day on the Calendar and see everywhere you went, in order — a map with every stop pinned, and a card for each memory with its place, time, and photos, all on one page.

A FEELING, NOT A SCORE
MomentMap never asks "how many stars?" Each memory gets its own feeling instead, because the same place can be unforgettable once and ordinary the next time, and a rating can't hold both — a memory can.

EASY TO SAVE, EASY TO SHARE
Saving the place itself is the easy part — share a link from Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Yelp, type a name, or let your photos suggest it, and organize it into Collections that make sense to you. Send a place or a whole collection to another MomentMap user just as easily. That's the infrastructure. The memory is the point.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your places and memories sync across your own devices with iCloud. No account, no login, no feed. Nothing you save is visible to anyone unless you explicitly share it — and sharing sends only the place, never your personal memories.

MomentMap isn't for finding places. It's for remembering what they meant.
